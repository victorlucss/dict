Hotkey
The shortcut you press anywhere to start and stop dictating.
Hotkey Mode
Toggle: tap once to start, tap again to stop. Push to talk: record only while you hold the key down.
Language
The language you'll speak. Not sure? Leave it on Auto-detect and Dict will figure it out from your voice.
Flow Mode
Presses Return for you after pasting, so chat messages send themselves.
Code Mode
Keeps symbols, casing, and syntax intact when you're dictating code.
Privacy Mode
Keeps everything on your Mac. Nothing goes to cloud LLMs, and your history isn't saved.
Engine
Which speech-to-text engine turns your voice into text. Whisper covers 90+ languages; Parakeet is faster and more accurate on its supported languages.
Whisper Model
The model that turns your speech into text. Bigger ones hear you more accurately, but take more space and run slower.

Parakeet Model

Microphone
Which mic Dict listens to. System Default follows your macOS sound settings.
Clean up with an LLM
Run each transcription through an LLM to fix grammar and punctuation and cut filler like "um" and "you know".
Correction level
How much the LLM changes your words, from a light touch-up to a full rewrite.
Provider
Who runs the cleanup. Local options keep your words on your Mac.
Model
Which model to use. Pick one from the list, or type the exact name your provider expects.
Endpoint
Where requests are sent. Filled in automatically for your provider. Only change it if you need to.
API Key
Your key for the cloud provider. Kept on your Mac and only sent to them.
Tone
How polished the final text reads. Pick what fits you.
Overlay Position
Where the recording bubble sits while you talk.
